# People Analytics Consulting Business Case Study
### Compensation, Performance & Employee Engagement Analyses

## Overview

This is a **People Analytics consulting** project for Pulse Diagnostics, a mid-sized healthcare organization facing increased competition for talent while maintaining an in-office operating model.

Using HRIS workforce data and employee engagement survey results, I conducted a diagnostic workforce analysis to evaluate whether the company's **"work-hard, play-hard" culture** was driving sustainable employee performance, engagement, and retention.

The outcome was an executive-level presentation with data-driven recommendations designed to support strategic HR decision-making.

---

## Business Challenge

Leadership wanted evidence to answer one key question:

> **Is the current culture delivering high performance at a sustainable cost?**

To investigate this, I analyzed workforce data across four dimensions:

- Compensation equity
- Performance management
- Employee engagement
- Time-off culture (PTO)

---

## Analytical Approach

Starting with a raw HRIS dataset, I:

- Cleaned and standardized workforce data
- Validated data quality and resolved inconsistencies
- Identified business problems and formulated analytical hypotheses
- Performed descriptive and diagnostic analysis using Excel
- Translated findings into executive-ready insights and recommendations

---

## Key Insights

The analyses uncovered several organizational risks:

- **Compensation inconsistencies** across office locations, suggesting potential salary band governance issues.
- **Performance rating compression**, limiting differentiation between high and average performers.
- **High employee engagement (81%)**, but **low comfort taking PTO (36%)**, indicating a culture that rewards performance more than recovery.
- Workforce concentration across three major offices, reducing cross-functional mobility in smaller locations.

Together, these findings suggested that while employees were highly committed to their work, the organization's operating model created long-term risks related to burnout, talent development, and retention.

---

## Business Recommendations

The project proposed practical actions for HR leadership, including:

- Standardize salary band governance across locations
- Improve performance calibration and manager coaching
- Normalize PTO through leadership behaviours and workload planning
- Prioritize development initiatives for Technology and Customer Service teams

These recommendations were organized into a **90-day implementation roadmap** with measurable success indicators.
